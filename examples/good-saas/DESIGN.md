# Design

> Generated by `project-starter-pack`. This brief covers **how the product is understood and used (UX)** before **how it looks (UI)**. Visual decisions answer to UX context, not the other way around.

---

## UX Foundation

### User knowledge

Engineering managers running biweekly retros. They have used EasyRetro, Notion, and FigJam. They know what columns are (Went well / Didn't / Actions) and they know what voting is. They do *not* want to learn a new metaphor. They want fewer clicks, better follow-through, and a tool that pushes back when they try to skip the writing phase.

Vocabulary they use: "retro", "items", "actions", "owners", "carryover", "blockers". Vocabulary to avoid: "stickies", "boards", "kudos", "celebrations", "pulse".

Known pain points the product owns:
- Action items disappear after the meeting.
- Half the team doesn't write anything before the call.
- The same blocker shows up in three retros and nobody noticed.

### Information architecture

Top-level navigation (sidebar, four entries):
- **Retros** — list of past + scheduled retros for the active team.
- **Actions** — open and recently-closed action items, filterable by owner.
- **Patterns** — items that have appeared in ≥3 retros (computed automatically).
- **Settings** — team, integrations (Slack, Linear, GitHub), billing.

Entities and relations:
- `Team` 1—n `Retro` 1—n `Item` 1—n `Vote`
- `Item` 0..1—1 `Action` 1—1 `Owner` (User)
- `Action` carries forward to the next `Retro` until `status = done | dropped`.

Routes:
- `/teams/:team` — redirects to active retro or list
- `/teams/:team/retros` — list
- `/teams/:team/retros/:retro` — single retro (the meeting surface)
- `/teams/:team/retros/:retro/write` — pre-meeting writing surface
- `/teams/:team/actions` — open actions across retros
- `/teams/:team/patterns` — recurring items

### User flows

**Pre-meeting writing** — trigger: retro is scheduled for today + scheduled-time minus 24h.
1. Each team member receives a single Slack DM with a link.
2. Link opens directly to the writing surface for that retro, scoped to that user.
3. User writes cards into three columns (Went well / Didn't / Actions).
4. Cards are private until the meeting starts, then they're revealed simultaneously to the whole team.
- Edge: user joins a team mid-cycle — they get the link only if the retro is ≥4 hours away.
- Empty state: "No cards yet — the team typically writes 3–5. What stood out this sprint?"
- Loading: skeleton shows the three columns immediately; cards stream in.
- Error: write failures save locally and retry; surface a "Saved locally — reconnecting" banner.

**Running the meeting** — trigger: facilitator clicks "Start retro".
1. All cards reveal at once, anonymized (author hidden).
2. Facilitator clusters by drag — keyboard accessible via `j`/`k`/`m`.
3. Voting opens — each user gets 5 dots, single-key voting (`v` to vote on hovered card).
4. Top-3 clusters open for discussion; timer per topic (default 6 min, adjustable).
5. For each cluster, facilitator captures one or more actions; each action requires an owner *before* the timer ends.
- Edge: meeting runs past time — a single nudge banner ("3 actions still need owners"); facilitator can defer to async, not skip.
- Empty state on the actions panel: "Every cluster needs at least one action or an explicit 'no action — discussed'."

**Post-meeting follow-through** — trigger: meeting ends.
1. Action items appear in the owner's `/actions` view and as a Slack DM.
2. Carryover: open actions from this retro pre-fill the next retro's "Carryover" column.
3. Owner marks `done` or `dropped` (with a one-line reason) before the next retro.
- Edge: owner leaves the team — actions auto-flag as orphaned; facilitator reassigns at next retro.

**Pattern detection (passive)** — trigger: an item is clustered into a theme that has ≥3 occurrences across the last 6 retros.
1. Pattern surfaces in the `/patterns` view with the cluster history.
2. No notification — patterns are read on-demand, not pushed.

### Interaction principles

- **Recognition over recall.** Owners are picked from a populated list, not typed.
- **Undo over confirm.** Deleting a card shows a 5-second undo toast; no "Are you sure?" dialog.
- **Keyboard-first for the meeting surface.** Every facilitation action has a single-key shortcut.
- **Progressive disclosure.** Settings stay collapsed until needed. Patterns are not in the default sidebar for teams under 6 retros.
- **System speaks the user's language.** "Carryover", "actions", "owners" — never "tickets", "tasks", or "epics".

### Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA across every route.
- Keyboard-only facilitation works end-to-end (see PRODUCT.md commitment).
- Visible focus rings on every interactive element; never `outline: none` without a replacement.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected — the card-reveal animation degrades to instant.
- Color is never the only signal — every accent-tagged item also has a label or icon.
- Screen-reader announces: meeting start, voting open, voting closed, time-up per topic.
- Reading level: 8th grade for product copy, 10th grade for settings/billing.

### UX Success metrics

- **Pre-meeting writing rate** ≥75% of team members per retro (instrumented per session).
- **Time-to-first-value** ≤8 minutes from sign-up to first card written.
- **Meeting-end action ownership** = 100%. Any retro that ends with unowned actions is a regression.
- **Action-item completion rate** ≥60% by the following retro.
- **Qualitative**: post-meeting one-question survey — "Was this retro useful?" — Likert 1–5; target median ≥4.
- **Regressions**: any of the above falling >10 points week-over-week, or any new modal interrupting the meeting flow.

## UI System

### Visual register

A studious notebook, not a dashboard. Closer to *Are.na* and the Baron Fig planner than to Linear or Notion. Quiet, warm, type-led; a single decisive accent for the live state of the meeting.

### Color strategy

**Strategy:** Restrained.

OKLCH, no pure black or white. One accent (deep evergreen) used on ≤8% of surface, reserved for live-meeting state and primary actions.

```
--bg            oklch(98% 0.01 95)     /* warm off-white paper */
--bg-elevated   oklch(96% 0.012 95)    /* card surface */
--fg            oklch(22% 0.02 250)    /* near-charcoal, cool tilt */
--fg-muted      oklch(45% 0.015 250)   /* secondary text */
--accent        oklch(38% 0.10 155)    /* deep evergreen */
--accent-fg     oklch(98% 0.005 155)   /* on-accent text */
--border        oklch(88% 0.01 95)     /* hairline */
--danger        oklch(45% 0.15 25)     /* not red-red — burnt sienna */
```

Dark mode: same hues, inverted lightness, accent shifts to `oklch(70% 0.10 155)` to keep ≥4.5:1 contrast on the elevated surface.

### Typography

- **Display**: Source Serif 4 (variable). Used for retro titles, the "Patterns" page, and the meeting timer when ≤2 minutes remain.
- **Body**: Inter (variable, optical sizing on). Everything else.
- **Mono**: JetBrains Mono. Used in the integrations panel for repo names and Slack channel IDs only.

Base size 16px / 1rem. Scale ratio 1.25 (major third). Body line-height 1.55. Measure cap 68ch on prose surfaces, 56ch in cards. Display headlines max 4 words when set in caps; otherwise sentence case.

### Spacing & layout

- Spacing scale: `4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64 96` (px). 8px base, with a 4px half-step for tight metadata clusters.
- Grid: 12-column on marketing, no grid in the app — content is left-anchored to a 720px reading column with a fixed 280px sidebar.
- Density: comfortable. The meeting surface tightens during voting (cards shrink to 220px wide) and relaxes during discussion (back to 320px).
- **Cards are allowed only on the writing and meeting surfaces.** The actions list, patterns view, and settings use hairline borders and spacing — no card stack for list items.

### Motion

- Default easing: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (expo-out).
- Durations: fast 120ms, base 220ms, slow 380ms.
- The single signature transition: card reveal at meeting start — staggered 40ms per card, 220ms each, on `transform: translateY(8px)` and `opacity`. Degrades to instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Never animate: layout properties (width, height, top, left), color of body text, focus rings.
- No bounce. No spring. No "delight" animations. The meeting is the product; motion serves it, not itself.

### Component primitives

The first 10 components define the system:

1. **Button** — square corners (2px radius), no shadow at rest, weight via type and color contrast. Two variants: `primary` (accent) and `quiet` (text + underline on hover).
2. **Card** — used only on writing/meeting surfaces. Hairline border, no shadow, 4px radius, 16px padding.
3. **Input** — bottom border only by default. Focus replaces the border with a 2px accent line. No box.
4. **Avatar** — 24px / 32px / 48px sizes. Initials over a generated muted swatch. Never photographic by default.
5. **Tag** — used for action status (`open`, `done`, `dropped`, `orphaned`). Hairline border + label. Color is supplementary to the label, never the only signal.
6. **Toast** — bottom-left, hairline border, 5-second timeout. Only used for undo and connection state.
7. **Sidebar nav item** — text-only, accent left-marker (3px) when active. No background fill.
8. **Modal** — used twice in the whole product: confirm-delete-team and confirm-delete-account. Nothing else.
9. **Timer** — large numeric display, switches to Source Serif 4 at ≤2 minutes remaining; never flashes, never beeps.
10. **Vote chip** — small accent dot; user's used dots fade to 30% opacity. No counter.

### UI Anti-patterns to avoid

Embedded from `guardrails/design-anti-patterns.md`, plus the project-specific bans below.

- No purple gradients; no neon-on-black; no gradient text.
- No pure `#000` or `#FFF` on body surfaces.
- No nested cards. No identical-card grids of features.
- No drop shadows as default elevation. No glassmorphism.
- No bounce easing. No animations on layout properties. No motion that ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No "delight" animations on critical paths.

**Project-specific bans:**
- No streak counters, badges, or "you wrote 5 weeks in a row!" patterns.
- No emoji in the product UI (allowed in user-typed card text only).
- No leaderboards. The retro is not a competition.
- No dashboards with hero numbers. The closest we have is the actions completion rate, shown as a small chip on the retros list.
- No celebratory confetti, ever.
